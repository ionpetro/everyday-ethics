# Everyday Ethics curriculum and design

## Goal

Help adults practice recognizing relevant facts, competing responsibilities, alternative actions and reasons to revise a decision. Start with familiar situations, not abstract terminology. Moral frameworks are useful lenses; they are not an algorithm that declares someone a good person.

## Scope

Twelve units / forty-eight lessons, progressing from honesty, uncertainty and perspectives through care, consent, fairness, promises, influence, digital life, speaking up, repair and integrated judgment. Each lesson has original semantic teaching items and a held-back transfer linked to a previously taught principle. Each unit ends with a boundary-sensitive roundup. Detailed lesson objectives and provenance are in foundations.md and practice.md.

## Learning mechanics

Unscored initial guess → explicit teaching and immediate checks → separately shuffled harder retrieval → held-back new case → older due material. The native engine divides lessons into short sittings; a lesson may take more than one sitting. Review uses six Leitner intervals. Weekends and freezes protect streaks. Optional quick recall covers neutral concepts, never timed moral judgments. Dilemmas carry no score and retain private reflection.

## Sources

E1: Markkula's Framework for Ethical Decision Making supports consideration of rights, justice, consequences, common good, virtue and care. E2: its introduction emphasizes context and judgment rather than a decision algorithm. E3: the ACM Code supports privacy, avoiding harm, honesty and accountability in computing. These are normative resources, not evidence that this app changes behavior. Authors label their practical synthesis and fictional cases accordingly.

- Original native content, source coverage, condition-dependent answer keys, explanations, transfer prerequisites and boundary cases verified.
- Working onboarding, lessons, feedback, progress/review, guide search, source library, dilemmas/reflections, theme and backup controls.
- No production sales curriculum, copied analytics, broken public repository claims or private source material.
- Automated engine/content/lab checks and build pass; representative desktop/mobile browser flows reviewed.
- An owner-private deployed Site with matching pushed source and packaged build.
